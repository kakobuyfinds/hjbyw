From discovery to decision

How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet helps organize candidate products. It is the beginning of your comparison: the seller listing, chosen variant and current agent rules determine whether an item can actually be ordered.

Build a shortlist

Search a product type and save its item ID. Compare similar listings by measurements, materials and intended use.

Check the source

Open the catalog entry and locate the original seller details. Confirm the exact color, size, quantity and variant price.

Confirm purchasing

Check your chosen agent’s current marketplace support, item restrictions and payment terms before funding an account.

Review and ship

If warehouse inspection is available, review your order’s photos and measurements. Then compare parcel routes and the complete delivery quote.

Practical buying checks

Look beyond the product price

  • Sizing: compare garment measurements with an item you own. For shoes, check the seller’s sizing method and available foot-length guidance.
  • QC photos: request clear views of the actual ordered item when the service is available. A stock photo cannot confirm what reaches a warehouse.
  • Returns: establish who pays return delivery and when a request must be made. Check the deadline before approving shipment.
  • Parcel cost: ask whether the route uses actual or volumetric weight and whether packaging changes affect the quote.

Clear answers

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product-discovery list used to compare finds and organize shopping links. This page pairs an independent guide with a small, searchable snapshot of the HipobuyFind catalog.

Is this an official ACBuy website?

No. This website is independent and does not operate ACBuy accounts, accept order payments or handle shipments. Its two main buttons lead to HipobuyFind.

Are these prices live, and is shipping included?

The displayed CNY prices are catalog references recorded on 19 November 2026. They do not update automatically and exclude shipping and fees. Confirm the current variant price and full order total before paying.

Can every catalog item be purchased through ACBuy?

Compatibility is not verified here. Marketplace support and product restrictions can change. Ask the agent through its official support channel whether it accepts the original seller link and exact item.

Does a listing include verified QC photos?

This directory does not certify QC coverage. Check what photos are available, whether they show the relevant variant, and whether they belong to your own order. Inspection photos do not establish authenticity or long-term durability.

What should I do if a product link stops working?

Search the item ID or product description in HipobuyFind. If a replacement listing appears, compare the seller, variant and price again instead of assuming it is the same offer.
